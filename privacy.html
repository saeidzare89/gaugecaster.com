Privacy policy

Updated 14 November 2026. A draft for GaugeCaster's pre-release versions, to be checked by someone qualified before GaugeCaster is offered to the public.

In short

GaugeCaster has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your recordings and projects stay on your device. GaugeCaster sends something to another service only when you ask it to (uploading an episode to your podcast host, for example), and then it sends it straight from your device to that service. Nothing passes through us, and we do not collect information about you or how you use the app.

What stays on your device

  • Your projects: recordings, edits, exports and episode details, in the projects folder you choose.
  • Your settings, including the details of your shows.
  • The keys and sign-ins for the podcast hosts and services you connect, in the app's own folder, kept apart from your projects and settings.

If your projects folder is one that a cloud service copies (such as OneDrive), that service receives what is in it, under its own terms. GaugeCaster warns you when it sees this.

Podcast hosts

When you connect a show to Podbean, Buzzsprout or Transistor and choose to upload an episode, GaugeCaster sends that host the episode's audio file, its title, description, season and number, date, picture and chapters, using the key or sign-in you gave it. The host's own privacy policy applies to what it receives: Podbean, Buzzsprout, Transistor. Removing the connection in Settings deletes the key from your device.

YouTube and your Google account

GaugeCaster uses YouTube API Services. When that feature is available and you choose to sign in with Google, GaugeCaster asks for permission to upload videos to your YouTube channel and to see the channel's name, so it can show which channel an episode goes to. It uses that permission only to upload the videos you choose, with the title, description and picture you gave them.

  • The permission Google gives GaugeCaster is kept on your device only. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • GaugeCaster does not read, keep or share any other information from your Google account or your channel.
  • You can take the permission back at any time on your Google account's permissions page, or by disconnecting in GaugeCaster's Settings.
  • By using GaugeCaster's YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's Privacy Policy applies to what Google receives.

GaugeCaster's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This website

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. It is served by GitHub Pages, which may record visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Email

If you write to help@gaugecaster.com, we use your address and what you send only to answer you, and do not share it.

Children

GaugeCaster is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

When this policy changes, the new version is posted here with its date.
