Record it, clean it up, put it out.

GaugeCaster is a recording studio for podcasts and narration. Each voice gets its own track, a few clicks make it sound better, and the finished episode goes to your podcast host at the right loudness, with its picture and chapters.

What it does

Record

Every voice on its own track

Two or more microphones at once, kept in step, or the inputs of one interface split onto separate tracks. Record the computer's own sound too.

Edit

Compact or Studio

Compact walks you from recording to sharing in four steps. Studio is a full timeline: split, trim, fade, ripple-delete, markers, a mixer, undo for everything.

Sound

Cleaner in a click

Gauge checks each take for noise, hum, harsh S sounds, pops and room echo and fixes what it can: Podcast, Narration and Singing starting points, or every stage by hand.

Read

From a script

A teleprompter that scrolls with you, a take per paragraph, the best take of each laid out in order. Retake a line without losing your place.

Play

A sound pad

Intros, stings and applause on keys 1 to 9 while you record. Music beds duck under your voice by themselves.

Publish

Straight to your host

MP3 or M4A at −16 LUFS (−19 for mono), with the episode's title, picture and chapters, uploaded to Podbean, Buzzsprout or Transistor.

Your recordings stay yours

No account, no cloud, no tracking. Projects live in a folder on your own device, and nothing leaves it unless you export, share or upload it. Privacy
