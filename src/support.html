Support

GaugeCaster has a guide built in: open Settings, then Help, and search for what you are doing. Each topic says where to find the controls in Compact and in Studio.

Write to us

help@gaugecaster.com

The same address is for anything else: trying a pre-release version, a feature you would like, or a question about privacy or the terms.

When something goes wrong

  • Which version you have (Settings, About) and on what: Windows, Android or Linux.
  • What you did, step by step, and what happened instead of what you expected.
  • A screenshot, if something looked wrong.

Please do not send recordings unless we ask for one: a short sample is usually enough.

Your projects are safe

Updating or uninstalling GaugeCaster never touches your projects. They are in the projects folder shown in Settings, Projects (on Windows, Gauge\Projects in your user folder unless you chose another).
